# Upload-it | Universal Operations Platform

**Subtract Architect Studios** · *Intelligence by Subtraction*

**One flexible foundation. Your organization. Your workflow. Your platform.**

Upload-it is a configurable operations and accountability platform concept for service businesses, property operations, contractors, inspection teams, municipalities, and larger enterprises.

Built from 25 years of firsthand service-industry experience, the vision is to replace fragmented paperwork, notes, and overly complicated industry-specific tools with an accessible, role-aware workspace that shows each person the information relevant to their work.

## What the platform is designed to offer

- **Industry-adaptable workflows:** terminology, records, dashboards, and processes tailored to different types of organizations.
- **Role-aware views:** relevant information for administrators, employees, contractors, inspectors, clients, and, where appropriate, the public.
- **Operational visibility:** work items, schedules, documents, progress, and accountability.
- **Evidence-based processes:** photos, receipts, corrective actions, and documented review.
- **Brand customization:** organization name, logo, colors, and appearance.
- **Public-service potential:** approved public-facing information to reduce routine status inquiries while protecting private records.
